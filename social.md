LinkedIn variation 1 founder launch
Today I am launching Figma Specs, an open-source Figma plugin for AI-first design handoff.
It converts any selected frame or component into visual spec frames on canvas for review, and compact structured YAML for coding agents like Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, and Cursor.
Why this matters: most handoffs to AI are noisy and expensive. With structured specs, teams often move from 80k+ raw tokens to around 20k depending on screen complexity.
Result: fewer retries, lower cost, faster shipping.

LinkedIn variation 2 problem and solution
A recurring issue in AI-assisted frontend delivery is feeding coding agents huge raw Figma context and hoping for good output.
I built Figma Specs to solve that.
