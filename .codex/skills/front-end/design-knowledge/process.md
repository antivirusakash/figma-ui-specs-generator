## Unblocking Techniques

When stuck on a design problem:

1. **Design the edge case first** — Constraints reveal the real problem
2. **Look at non-digital equivalents** — Physical world has solved many UX problems
3. **Remove elements until it breaks** — Find the essential
4. **Ask "what would the lazy solution be?"** — Sometimes simple is right

---

## Research vs Instinct

| Use Research For | Use Instinct For |
|------------------|------------------|
| "What" and "whether" | "How" to solve |
| High stakes / low reversibility | Craft details users can't articulate |
| Challenging your assumptions | — |

> Users identify problems; designers solve them.

---

## Anti-Patterns (Junior Mistakes)

| Mistake | Takes Years to Unlearn |
|---------|------------------------|
| Happy path only | Forgetting errors, edge cases, empty states |
| Copying patterns without understanding context | Cargo-culting from Dribbble |
| Optimizing for first impression | Looks great in review, annoying on day 30 |

---

## Decision Framework

When facing a design decision:

1. **What are we optimizing for?** (speed, clarity, flexibility)
2. **What are we explicitly giving up?** (tradeoffs)
3. **How would we know if we're wrong?** (validation)
4. **What's the cost to change later?** (reversibility)

---

## Review Questions

Before considering a design done:

- What edge cases haven't been designed?
- What will engineers have to assume?
- What breaks if this is 10x bigger/smaller?
- What's the day-30 experience, not day-1?
