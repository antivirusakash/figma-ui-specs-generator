## Buyer Segments

| Segment | Behavior |
|---------|----------|

| Growth (10-100) | Scale leadership, preserve knowledge as team grows |
| Enterprise (100+) | Capture institutional knowledge, reduce key-person risk |

### By Role

|------|-------------|

| CTO | Knowledge preservation, reduce dependency |

## UX Implications

| Finding | Design Implication |
|---------|-------------------|
| "Presence multiplied" language | Position twin as extension of self, not replacement |
| Fear of things falling through cracks | Surface proactive alerts, not just reactive logs |
| Context switching pain | Design for rapid context recall, not just storage |
| Key-person dependency fear | Emphasize knowledge capture during onboarding |
| Trust concerns (implicit) | Progressive autonomy, not day-one full delegation |

---

## Product Implications

| Finding | Product Implication |
|---------|---------------------|
| Chief of Staff as common frame | Model product around CoS workflows |
| Meeting proxy explicitly requested | Meeting attendance is core, not peripheral feature |
| Knowledge retention for HR | Consider enterprise knowledge capture as use case |
| Consultant segment notable | Self-serve for solo, team features for growth |
| "Clone" language common | Twin identity/persona configuration is table stakes |
